# Power-BI-Financial-Performance-Analysis
Project Overview
This project presents an interactive financial performance dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. It analyses business performance through key financial metrics, sales trends, profitability, and segment-level contributions.The dashboard transforms raw financial data into meaningful visual insights to support data-driven business decisions.

Objectives
Analyse overall sales and profit performance.
Track year-over-year sales growth.
Evaluate profit margins and profitability trends.
Compare revenue contribution across business segments.
Identify top-performing products and countries.

Tools & Technologies
Power BI – Dashboard development and data visualization
Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
DAX – Calculated measures and financial KPIs
Data Modelling – Organising data for analysis

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Sales
Total Profit
Profit Margin
Year-over-Year (YoY) Sales Growth

Key Business Insights
Strong Sales Growth: Sales increased by approximately 249.46%, rising from $26.42M in 2013 to $92.31M in 2014.
Profitability Trend: Profit margin declined slightly from 14.68% in 2013 to 14.10% in 2014 despite significant revenue growth.
Segment Contribution: Government and Small Business segments together contributed approximately 80% of total sales.

Dashboard Features
Interactive KPI cards
Year-wise sales and profit analysis
Sales and profit trend visualizations
Business segment performance analysis
Product-level sales comparison
Country-wise profitability analysis
Interactive filters and slicers
